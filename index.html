Intrinsic Evaluations

Visualization of the 200 most frequent words

This plot shows a 2D projection of the 200 most frequent words in the dataset based on their embeddings. Words with similar meanings or functions are located close to each other. Clusters can be observed for numbers, pronouns, and directional words like south and north, as well as cluster words such as german, english and french.

Clusterization

This plot shows the result of KMeans clustering applied to Word2Vec embeddings of selected semantic groups. Words are grouped into clusters that reflect their semantic similarity. An interesting observation is that the word “may” does not appear within the months cluster, likely due to its ambiguity as a modal verb. Additionally, colors and animals clusters are slightly mixed, they are located close to each other. Overall, the clustering is good, as most words from the same semantic categories are grouped together in the embedding space.

Extrinsic Evaluation

Evaluation was performed on three datasets: AG News, IMDB, and DBpedia. AG News is a dataset of news articles with four topic classes, used to assess how well the model captures topical information. IMDB consists of movie reviews with binary sentiment labels (positive/negative) and is used to evaluate whether embeddings capture emotional polarity. DBpedia is a multi-class dataset of structured knowledge entities, designed to test the model's ability to represent more complex semantic information. For all datasets, texts were represented using three types of embeddings: random vectors, TF-IDF, and trained Word2Vec embeddings. Document representations were obtained by averaging word vectors, and a Logistic Regression classifier was trained on top of each representation. The results show that Word2Vec significantly outperforms random embeddings and approaches the performance of TF-IDF, achieving competitive results across all tasks.
